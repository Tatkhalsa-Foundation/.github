
# Tatkhalsa Foundation

**Tatkhalsa Foundation** is a Section 8 Non-Profit Organization dedicated to humanitarian relief, emergency blood dispatch, and Sikh digital heritage preservation.

- **Legal Status:** Section 8 NGO
- **CIN:** `U88900PB2023NPL059225`
- **NITI Aayog Darpan ID:** `PB/2026/0998693`
- **Registration:** Punjab, India

---

## Key Initiatives & Platforms

### 🌐 Official Web Platform — [tatkhalsa.in](https://tatkhalsa.in)
Our central digital hub for humanitarian relief, verified Donor & Sevadar ID portals, Section 80G tax-exempt donation verification, and community welfare initiatives.

### 🩸 Blood On Call 2.0
A 24/7 voluntary blood donor directory and emergency dispatch network across Punjab with instant WhatsApp hospital requisitions.

### 📖 Tatkhalsa Web Pothi — [pothi.tatkhalsa.in](https://pothi.tatkhalsa.in)
A digital Gurbani and historical Sikh scripture portal featuring Puratan handwritten Gurmukhi fonts, Larivaar display, and Nitnem.
