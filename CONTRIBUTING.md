# Contributing to Tatkhalsa Foundation

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Tatkhalsa Foundation projects! We welcome contributions from developers, researchers, translators, and community members who share our mission.

## Code of Conduct

All contributors and maintainers are expected to abide by our [Code of Conduct](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Please report unacceptable behavior to `info@tatkhalsa.in`.

## Pull Request Workflow

1. **Fork the repository** and create a new branch from `main` (e.g., `feat/my-feature` or `fix/issue-description`).
2. **Make your changes** cleanly and keep commits focused and descriptive.
3. **Test your changes** locally where applicable and ensure existing checks pass.
4. **Open a Pull Request** against `main` with a clear description of the problem solved, changes made, and any relevant issue references.
5. **Address review feedback** promptly. Maintainers will review both technical quality and domain accuracy before merging.

## Gurbani & Panthic Accuracy Review

For any contributions involving Gurbani text, Gurmukhi orthography, transliterations, translations, Sikh history, or Panthic/Rehat Maryada references:

- **Primary Sources:** Verify all Gurbani verses, Shabads, and historical citations against authentic primary sources before submitting.
- **No Unverified Edits:** Do not alter Gurmukhi spelling, *lagaa-maatraa* (vowel markings), or *vishraam* (punctuation) without documented scholarly justification.
- **Maintainer Sign-off:** All Gurbani and Panthic content changes require explicit accuracy review and approval from designated Tatkhalsa Foundation reviewers prior to merge.

## Reporting Issues

- For general bugs or feature requests, open an issue in the relevant repository with clear reproduction steps.
- For security vulnerabilities, **do not** open a public issue; follow our [Security Policy](./SECURITY.md).
